Early intervention · Sarjapur

Support development early, through play and everyday routines.

Early intervention brings communication, movement, play and participation together during the years when young children are developing rapidly. The plan should reflect the child, not a fixed programme.

Ask about early intervention

Parents often contact us about

  • Delayed speech or limited communication
  • Limited shared play or interaction
  • Motor or sensory concerns
  • Difficulty joining everyday routines
  • Questions about overall development

What early intervention may include

Communication

Building understanding, expression and useful communication in daily interactions.

Play and connection

Supporting shared attention, engagement, imitation and age-appropriate play.

Movement and participation

Working on relevant motor, sensory and everyday participation skills.

Parent guidance

Helping caregivers understand goals and use practical strategies within real routines.

Questions parents ask about early intervention

Do we need to wait for a diagnosis?

No. If you have developmental concerns, you can begin with a conversation and appropriate assessment.

Does early intervention mean multiple therapies?

Not automatically. The relevant professionals and goals depend on the child's needs after assessment.

How are parents involved?

Parents share what happens in everyday routines and can be guided on practical ways to support skills outside sessions.
