Special education · Sarjapur

Make learning accessible, practical and understandable.

Special education starts with the child's current learning profile. Goals can include early concepts, attention, classroom participation, school readiness and practical independence.

Ask about learning support

Parents often contact us about

  • Difficulty learning early concepts
  • Challenges with attention or task engagement
  • School-readiness concerns
  • Difficulty following classroom routines
  • Need for individualised learning support

What special education may work on

Foundational learning

Early literacy, numeracy, concepts and learning readiness at an accessible pace.

Attention and task skills

Engagement, following instructions and building learning routines.

School readiness

Classroom expectations, transitions, participation and early academic demands.

Practical independence

Connecting learning to everyday routines and functional skills.

Questions parents ask

Can special education help with school readiness?

Yes, when support is needed with early concepts, attention, routines or classroom participation.

Will the work match what my child is doing at school?

School material and teacher observations can be considered, but the plan should reflect the child's actual learning level.

How are goals reviewed?

Progress is reviewed against specific learning goals, with parent observations and school feedback included when useful.
